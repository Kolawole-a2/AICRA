## Notes on Identifier Types

### DOIs (Digital Object Identifiers)
- **Format:** `10.xxxx/xxxxx`
- **Access:** `https://doi.org/[DOI]`
- **Available for:** Published journal articles, conference proceedings (modern), some books
- **Not available for:** arXiv preprints, older conference papers, frameworks, software

### arXiv IDs
- **Format:** `arXiv:YYYY.NNNNN` or `arXiv:YYMM.NNNNN`
- **Access:** `https://arxiv.org/abs/[arXiv ID]`
- **Note:** arXiv preprints do not have DOIs but are permanently archived and verifiable

### ISBNs (International Standard Book Numbers)
- **Format:** ISBN-13: `978-xxxxxxxxxxxxx` or ISBN-10: `xxxxxxxxxx`
- **Verification:** Library catalogs, ISBN search engines, publisher websites
- **Available for:** Published books

### URLs
- **For:** Frameworks, software repositories, online resources
- **Note:** May change over time; DOIs are preferred when available
